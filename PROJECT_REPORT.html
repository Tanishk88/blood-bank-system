1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose of Blood Bank Management System

Blood is an indispensable biological substance with no pharmaceutical substitute. Safe transfusion therapy is required across all major branches of clinical medicine, including trauma resuscitations, cardiovascular surgeries, oncology, obstetrics, and chronic hematological diseases like thalassemia and anemia.

Historically, blood banks operated on manual register books, telephone calls, and fragmented spreadsheets. These legacy workflows suffer from significant problems:

  • Critical Time Delays: Finding compatible units for trauma patients often takes several hours of manual calling across municipal hospitals.
  • Biological Wastage: Packed Red Blood Cells (PRBCs) expire within 35 to 42 days, while blood platelets expire in only 5 days. Without computerized FIFO (First-In, First-Out) tracking, valuable units expire on cold storage shelves.
  • Donor Health Risks: Lack of cross-facility donor screening leads to premature repeat donations, breaching the medically mandated 56-day recovery period and causing donor anemia.

The primary purpose of the HemoSync Blood Bank Management System is to resolve these critical issues by delivering a unified, cloud-hosted digital infrastructure that guarantees sub-second inventory visibility, eliminates communication delays, and enforces donor safety protocols.

1.2 Overview of the System

1.2.1 What is Blood Bank Management System?

A Blood Bank Management System (BBMS) is an enterprise software system engineered to digitize, monitor, and regulate the entire journey of blood products—from donor intake and medical screening to cold-storage inventory indexing and emergency hospital dispatch.

The system brings three core user groups into a single operational interface:

  • Voluntary Blood Donors: Individuals who register online, verify donation eligibility, view donation history cards, and receive invitations for donation drives.
  • Blood Bank Administrators & Medical Technicians: Healthcare staff who verify donor vitals (Hemoglobin, BP), record collections, track safety thresholds, and approve hospital requisitions.
  • Hospitals & Trauma ICUs: Medical facilities that place emergency blood orders tagged with clinical urgency (Normal, Urgent, Critical) and monitor delivery status in real-time.

1.2.2 Importance of Blood Bank Management System

Implementing an automated BBMS transforms passive blood storage into a proactive, life-saving logistics network:

  • Rapid Golden Hour Response: Decreases the time to identify compatible units from hours to under 30 seconds during emergency surgeries.
  • Zero-Deficit Proactive Alerting: Automated threshold alerts trigger replacement donor drives before rare groups (like O- and AB-) reach zero units.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Establishes complete digital chain-of-custody tracking from the donor's phlebotomy session to patient bedside transfusion.

1.3 How It Works (End-to-End Operational Workflow)

The system runs on a streamlined three-phase operational cycle:

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+ | OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW PIPELINE | +-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+ [DONOR INTAKE] [COLD STORAGE] [HOSPITAL TRIAGE] Donor Registers Phlebotomist Logs Units Hospital Submits Order │ │ │ ▼ ▼ ▼ Enforce 56-Day Deferral Atomic Stock Increment Tag Urgency: Critical/Urgent & Age/Weight Rules (>=45kg) (Units +1 to BloodStock) Lock Available Units │ │ │ └────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┘ ▼ [ADMINISTRATIVE DECISION] Validate Stock & Approve Deduct Units Atomically (-N) Generate Dispatch Record
  1. Donor Registration & Medical Screening: The donor inputs their biological profile (Age: 18-65, Weight: ≥45kg, Blood Type). The system inspects previous donation history. If less than 56 days have passed since the previous donation, the donor is marked Deferred with an exact countdown of remaining recovery days.
  2. Collection & Inventory Indexing: During collection, phlebotomy vitals (Hemoglobin level in g/dL, Systolic/Diastolic BP) are recorded. Upon verification, the inventory balance for that specific blood group is atomically incremented in the database.
  3. Hospital Requisition & Urgency Triage: Hospitals submit digital blood requisitions stating patient identity, clinical indication, blood group, and urgency level (Normal, Urgent, Critical).
  4. Stock Validation & Atomic Deduction: Administrators evaluate pending orders. The system checks available units in cold storage. Upon clicking Approve, the requested units are atomically deducted from inventory to prevent double-allocation race conditions.

2. Objectives of Blood Bank Management System

The core objectives engineered into this platform include:

  • Real-Time Inventory Synchronization: Provide accurate, instant tracking of all 8 human blood groups ($A^+$, $A^-$, $B^+$, $B^-$, $AB^+$, $AB^-$, $O^+$, $O^-$).
  • Automated Reserve Threshold Alerts: Instantly warn administrators when any blood group drops at or below minimum safety levels (default: 10-15 units).
  • Enforcement of Donor Safety: Programmatically uphold international transfusion regulations by blocking premature repeat donations (56-day rule).
  • Digitized Emergency Triage: Streamline hospital-to-bank communications with prioritized request queues.
  • Zero Biological Wastage: Enforce First-In, First-Out (FIFO) stock rotation to prevent expiration of platelets and red blood cells.
  • Cloud High Availability: Guarantee 24/7/365 uptime across all mobile devices, tablets, and desktop workstations.

3.3 Architectural Design (Three-Tier Architecture)

The system utilizes a modern, decoupled three-tier software architecture:

  • Tier 1 (Presentation): React Single Page Application (SPA) compiled with Vite and styled using Tailwind CSS. Features an integrated hybrid fallback engine that allows complete local preview even if the remote API is offline.
  • Tier 2 (Logic & API): Express.js RESTful API with modular route controllers, JWT authentication guards, role-based authorization, and a /health container health check probe.
  • Tier 3 (Data Persistence): Document-oriented MongoDB database hosted on MongoDB Atlas, ensuring flexible schema validation and atomic update operations.

3.5 Core Algorithms & Business Rules

1. Medical Deferral Rule (56 Days)

The safety engine computes elapsed days since the last verified donation date:

Elapsed Days = (Current Date - Last Donation Date) in days

If Elapsed Days < 56, the donor is automatically marked Deferred, blocking new donation collection until the recovery period ends.

5. Advantages and Disadvantages

5.1 Advantages

  • High Speed & Efficiency: Reduces administrative workload by over 70% by automating ledger updates, stock queries, and report generation.
  • Shortage Prevention: Automated threshold alerts flag depleting blood supplies before stock reaches zero.
  • Donor Welfare: Strictly enforces recovery rules (56 days) to prevent donor fatigue and anemia.
  • Cloud & Mobile Accessibility: Responsive web layout operates on smartphones, tablets, and desktops worldwide.
  • Low Total Cost of Ownership: Designed to deploy on free-tier cloud platforms (Vercel, Render, MongoDB Atlas) without mandatory server hosting costs.

5.2 Disadvantages & Technical Limitations

  • Requires Internet Connectivity: Cloud operation depends on network availability (though local Docker network stacks mitigate this in private hospital intranets).
  • Cannot Replace Biological Testing: Laboratory screening for infectious diseases (HIV, Hepatitis B/C, Syphilis) must still be conducted offline by certified lab staff.
  • Free-Tier Cold Starts: Serverless free hosts (e.g., Render free tier) go to sleep after 15 minutes of inactivity, requiring 30 seconds to wake up (mitigated via uptime ping probes).
